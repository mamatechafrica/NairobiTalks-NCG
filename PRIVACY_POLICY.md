# Privacy Policy & Do No Harm Statement

## Privacy Policy

NairobiTalks is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all users. We collect only the minimum personal data necessary for platform functionality and do not sell or share user data with third parties.

- **Data Collection:** Only information required for account creation, participation, and platform improvement is collected.
- **Data Use:** Data is used solely for the purposes of community engagement, platform improvement, and communication with users.
- **Data Protection:** All data is stored securely, with access restricted to authorized personnel. Encryption and best practices are used to safeguard information.
- **User Rights:** Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data at any time.
- **Cookies:** NairobiTalks uses cookies only for essential session management and security.

## Do No Harm Statement

NairobiTalks is designed and operated with a commitment to do no harm. We strive to:

- Protect all users from discrimination, harassment, and abuse.
- Ensure that platform features do not enable or facilitate harm to individuals or communities.
- Regularly review and update security and privacy practices.
- Respond promptly to any reports of misuse or harm.

## Non-Discrimination

NairobiTalks does not discriminate on the basis of race, ethnicity, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. All users are welcome and treated with respect.

## Contact

For privacy concerns or to report a problem, please contact the project maintainers via the contact information in this repository.
