Improve Public Transport in Westlands

Westlands Transportation

Add more bus routes and dedicated lanes to reduce commute times for thousands of commuters traveling between Westlands and the city center. This would significantly improve accessibility for workers and students in the area.

Expected Benefits: Reduced traffic congestion, faster commute times, lower transportation costs for residents, reduced carbon emissions from fewer vehicles on the road.
