# NairobiTalks Governance Charter

## Purpose

This document outlines the governance structure and decision-making processes for the NairobiTalks project to ensure transparency, accountability, and inclusive participation.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Project Maintainers:** Oversee project direction, review contributions, and manage releases.
- **Contributors:** Propose features, report issues, and submit code/documentation.
- **Community Members:** Participate in discussions, provide feedback, and help shape priorities.

## Decision-Making

- Major decisions are made by consensus among maintainers, with input from the community.
- Disagreements are resolved through discussion, and if needed, by a majority vote of maintainers.
- All decisions and rationales are documented in GitHub issues or pull requests.

## Meetings and Communication

- Regular meetings (virtual or in-person) may be held for planning and review.
- All meeting notes and decisions are published in the repository.
- Communication channels include GitHub Issues, Discussions, and project email.

## Code of Conduct

All participants must adhere to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Amendments

This charter may be amended by consensus of the maintainers, with notice to the community.

For questions or suggestions, please open an issue or contact the maintainers.
